What does a website cost?
@@COST_FAQ@@

Third-party costs such as your domain, hosting, paid plugins, and subscriptions are separate. You’ll receive a scope and quote before work starts.

What do I need to provide?

Your business details, services, contact information, logo if you have one, and any text or photos you want to use. Please only supply materials you have permission to use. We’ll identify any gaps together before starting.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the number of pages, features, how ready your content is, and how quickly feedback arrives. I’ll confirm my availability and discuss a realistic timeline with you when we agree on the scope.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. Send your current website and explain what’s no longer working for you. I’ll review the pages, platform, and access needed before proposing a refresh or rebuild.

Are hosting and the domain included?

No. Domain registration, hosting, paid plugins, and other subscription fees are separate from development. We’ll discuss suitable options and ongoing responsibilities before launch.

What happens after launch?

You can request updates as needed. I’ll confirm the scope, price, and availability before starting. New pages, features, redesigns, and complex changes receive a separate quote.

@@DEFECT_FAQ@@
Can I edit the site myself?

It depends on the platform we choose. A custom static website does not include a built-in content editor. If you need to make your own changes, tell me upfront so we can scope a suitable editing setup and any associated costs.

Who owns the domain, accounts, and finished work?

My proposed approach is for you to hold the domain and hosting accounts where practical, with developer access for me as needed. Ownership and transfer of custom deliverables after payment, plus any third-party licenses, must be confirmed in our written agreement before work starts.
